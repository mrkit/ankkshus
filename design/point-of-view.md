# Point of view

Draft written by Claude on 2026-10-25 from Anthony's answers in a working

## Who this is to its users

A place for two people, a brother and a sister, to keep up with each other
between visits. He built it for her. Both of them write in it.

## What they care about that others ignore

That the check-in is read by someone who loves her, not scored by a product.
That a note from the other one is the whole reason to open the site. That it
feels made by hand, by him, with a joke in the name, and not like an app.

## The one choice only this brand would make

It treats a clinical instrument (the Burns checklist) as a move on a family
game board: dated, kept, looked back on together, and never graded. The two
of them are players at the same table, each with a dragon for a piece.

## Their world (Anthony, 2026-10-25)

A big board-game family. They played Dungeons & Dragons once. His sister
loves dragons, different kinds of them.

## Rejections

- 2026-10-25, "the almanac": check-ins drawn as weather over weeks. Rejected
  by Anthony after it was chosen: the family's shared world is board games,
  not weather, and his sister loves dragons. Do not offer weather again.
- 2026-10-25, not chosen in the same round: "the fridge door" (taped notes
  and magnets), "the seasons folio" (check-ins as botanical plates), and
  the plain wellness-app standard.
